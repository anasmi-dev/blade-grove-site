Blade Grove Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the iOS game Blade Grove (bundle ID dev.anasmi.bladegrove) handles information. Blade Grove is developed by anasmi-dev, an individual developer (“I”, “me”).

Summary

  • Blade Grove has no accounts and no servers of its own.
  • Your game data stays on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.
  • I do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, best scores and unlocks are stored only on your device (using iOS UserDefaults). This data is not sent to me. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blade Grove uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show rewarded and interstitial ads. Google may collect and use information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • IP address and coarse (approximate) location
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads viewed or tapped
  • Diagnostic information

Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement and, where permitted, personalization. Google’s handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Consent and your choices

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK, and US states with privacy laws), Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choices at any time in the game from Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices.

You can turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchase

Blade Grove offers an optional “Remove Ads” in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. I do not receive your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple’s Game Center handles leaderboards. That data is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Sale of data

I do not sell your personal information.

Children

Blade Grove is intended for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights and controls

  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Change your ad consent choices in the game from Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/anasmi-dev/blade-grove-site/issues.
