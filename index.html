FAQ

How do I restore Remove Ads?

If you bought Remove Ads and reinstalled the game or switched devices, open Settings → Restore Purchases in the game while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices in the game to review or change your consent. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
